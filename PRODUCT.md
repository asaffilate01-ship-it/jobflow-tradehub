# TraderOS PRODUCT.md

Users: Tradespeople, customers, quote/dispatch operators and materials delivery drivers.

Purpose: Job discovery, scoped quotes, scheduling, evidence uploads, trade assignments, materials and payments.

Visual truth: Current src/index.css uses slate-blue HSL 216 62% 20% primary, warm orange accent HSL 29 78% 52% and dedicated dark states; prioritise legible trade operations.

Critical product constraints: Real job/quote price, scope and exclusions, dates and contractor identities must be clear. Distinguish estimates from accepted quotes, verified licence from self-description, and paid from requested payments.

Data comes from canonical database/domain sources. No design or AI-agent action may imply an approved payment, deployment, compliance decision or signed-off project without a verified event and owner.
