## Craftvaro differentiation

Craftvaro should not compete as another broad lead directory. Its defensible loop is:

1. A customer submits text plus photos or video.
2. Safety-first AI returns possibilities, uncertainty, prohibited actions, a cost range and the required trade.
3. Deterministic checks enforce trade, coverage, subscription, availability, insurance and regulated credentials.
4. Up to four eligible paid members receive a redacted opportunity containing only the safe evidence and postcode sector.
5. Offers include labour, materials, ETA, duration, assumptions, exclusions and warranty.
6. The customer accepts one offer; other invitations close automatically.
7. The job moves into CRM, evidence, milestone, document and Dokuvera workflows.
8. Only the completed awarded job can create a public review.

The AI should explain why each trader matched and never invent availability, credentials, ratings or prices. Model output interprets the request; database rules decide eligibility.

## Mobile experience standard

- One prominent search/AI action above the fold.
- Horizontally scrollable trade categories and a compact expandable filter sheet.
- Large tap targets, short evidence labels, honest empty states and no desktop-only hover actions.
- Member and unclaimed-directory cards must be visually distinct.
- Rating must include review count; zero reviews displays **New member**, never `5.0`.
- Provider cards show location coverage, availability, insurance, credential and the reason for the AI match.
- Contact is hidden on imported profiles. The primary action is **Claim this profile**.

## Germany launch controls

The Handwerksordnung and its Anlage A define regulated crafts and registration requirements. Before German launch, map each Craftvaro trade to the applicable German category and require Handwerksrolle or other competent-authority evidence where legally required: [Handwerksordnung](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hwo/BJNR014110953.html) and [Anlage A](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hwo/anlage_a.html).

Do not treat a UK credential as equivalent to a German qualification. Store credential authority, reference, permitted work categories, expiry, verification timestamp and verifier provenance separately.
